# Acme Corp Vendor Risk Management Standard

## Why vendors are assessed

Every outside company that stores, processes or can reach our data is a vendor. A vendor breach is a breach of our data,
so each vendor is assessed before the contract is signed and again every year.

## Vendor tiers

- **Tier 1**: the vendor handles Restricted data or runs a system the business cannot work without. Examples are the payment
  processor and the cloud provider.
- **Tier 2**: the vendor handles Confidential data. Examples are the marketing platform and the recruiting tool.
- **Tier 3**: the vendor has no access to company data, such as a catering supplier.

## What each tier must provide

Tier 1 vendors must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report and complete the full security questionnaire SQ-40. Tier 2
vendors complete the short questionnaire SQ-12. Tier 3 vendors need no assessment. A report older than 12 months counts as
expired.

## Exceptions

A business owner who wants to use a vendor that fails the assessment must file exception form VR-7. The exception needs
the signature of the Chief Information Security Officer, lasts at most six months, and is listed in the risk register.

## Incidents and exit

A vendor must tell us about a breach that affects our data within 48 hours of discovering it. When a contract ends, the
vendor must return or delete our data within 30 days and confirm in writing that it has done so.
