# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

## Approval

Expenses up to 500 dollars are approved by the employee's manager. Expenses from 501 to 5,000 dollars need the department
head. Anything above 5,000 dollars needs the Chief Financial Officer before the money is spent.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for every expense above 25 dollars. Submit the claim within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than
90 days are not paid.

## Travel

Book flights at least 14 days ahead through the company travel tool. Economy class is the default; flights longer than six
hours may be booked in premium economy. Hotels are capped at 220 dollars a night in major cities and 150 dollars elsewhere.
Use a rental car only when public transport is not practical.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance when travelling is 70 dollars. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Client entertainment must be approved in
advance and the guests' names written on the claim.

## Personal expenses

Fines, personal purchases, airline upgrades paid for by the employee, and mini-bar charges are not reimbursed. Paying with a
personal card for company purchases is allowed only if the corporate card is unavailable.

## Questions

Questions about a claim go to the finance helpdesk. Claims are paid on the 15th and the last day of every month.
