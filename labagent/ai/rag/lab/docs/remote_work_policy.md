# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week with their manager's approval. Roles that need physical access to
hardware, such as the data centre team, are not eligible. Eligibility is reviewed every six months.

## Working from another country

Working from outside the country of employment is allowed for up to 30 calendar days in a year. It needs written approval
from the manager and from the Legal team, because tax and employment rules differ by country. Approval request form HR-12
must be filed at least 14 days before travel. Employees may not work from countries on the sanctions list kept by Legal.

## Equipment and network

The company provides one laptop and one monitor. Personal laptops must not be used for company work. At home, use the
company VPN whenever you reach internal systems, and never connect to internal systems from a public computer. Home routers
should use WPA3 or WPA2 with a password of at least 12 characters.

## Home office allowance

Remote employees receive a stipend of 50 dollars per month for internet and phone costs, paid with the monthly payroll.
A one-time furniture allowance of 400 dollars is available in the first year. Receipts are required for the furniture
allowance but not for the monthly stipend.

## Security at home

Lock the screen whenever you leave your desk. Do not let family members use the work laptop. Calls about Confidential
or Restricted data must not take place where others can listen.
